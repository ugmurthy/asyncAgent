# Progress Log - Remove Goals and Runs Management

## Codebase Patterns
- Route registration in server.ts uses `fastify.register(routeHandler, { prefix: '/api/v1', ...options })`
- Routes can accept additional options like `scheduler` or `llmProvider` passed during registration
- When removing schema tables with FK relationships, also update dependent tables and relations
- When removing schema tables used by files slated for later removal, stub out those files to pass typecheck
